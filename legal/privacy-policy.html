Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-13 · applies to everyone

This Privacy Policy explains how Porchlight Pet & Home Care ("Porchlight," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use our website, client and sitter portals, forms, and booking tools (the "Service"). We built Porchlight on trust, and your home details deserve it.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact information: name, email address, phone number, street address, and the town you live in.
  • Pet information: names, species, breed, age, weight, health notes, medications, feeding instructions, temperament, vaccination records (including photos and PDFs of certificates), veterinarian and emergency clinic details, and photos of your animals.
  • Home information: how we get in (lockbox, door, garage and alarm codes, keys, Wi-Fi details), parking, home-care notes, house rules, and which animals and people are in the home.
  • Booking and payment information: the services and dates you book, tips, and the payment method you choose. We record payments made by Venmo, Zelle, check, cash or card. If card payments are on, a payment processor (Stripe) collects your card details and we do not see or store your full card number.
  • Sitter information: your application, work history, references, photo, availability, service area, payout details, and the results of your background check.
  • Consent records: the date and time you accepted a policy, which version you accepted, the checkbox text, and your email, IP address and browser or device type.
  • Messages and anything else you send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Visit check-in and check-out: when a Sitter starts or ends a visit in the portal, we may record the time and the device's location so you can see that the visit happened.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser, device and pages visited, kept to run and secure the Service.
  • Address suggestions: when you type an address, the box may use Google to suggest addresses and to estimate drive times (see "Who we share with").

We do not use your information to sell advertising and we do not sell your personal information.

2. How we use information

  • To set up your account, schedule visits, send reminders and visit reports, and run payments and payouts.
  • To match Owners with Sitters, and to give a Sitter the information needed to do an assigned visit.
  • To keep animals, homes and people safe, handle emergencies, and review complaints.
  • To screen Sitters, including with a background check.
  • To keep records of what you agreed to and to meet our legal obligations.
  • To communicate with you about your bookings, by text message and email, and with your permission about offers and news.
  • To run, improve and secure the Service.

3. Who sees your information

  • Sitters see an Owner's name, address, phone, codes, and pet and home details only for a visit that is assigned to them and confirmed, and until 3 days after the visit. Before that, open requests show only the town, the dates, and the animals.
  • Owners see a Sitter's first name and last initial, photo and short bio. After booking, you may see more information that the Sitter has chosen to share.
  • Porchlight staff can see information they need to do their jobs.
  • Service providers that help us run Porchlight, including: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting), Google (address suggestions and drive times), Resend (email delivery, when turned on), Stripe (card payments, when turned on), and a background-check company (for Sitter screening). They may use your information only to provide services to us.
  • Veterinarians and emergency clinics, when we need to care for your animal or confirm vaccination records (only with your permission for record checks).
  • Authorities or others if required by law, to respond to legal process, to protect safety, or to enforce our agreements.
  • A new owner of Porchlight, if the business is sold or merged, under the same promises.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

4. Text messages and email

By giving us your mobile number and agreeing to text messages, you allow us to text you about your bookings, visits and account. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to opt out of texts, though we may still send required booking messages by email or phone. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in and you can unsubscribe at any time.

5. Photos and visit reports

Photos and videos taken during visits are shared with you in the portal and are visible to you, your Sitter, and Porchlight staff. We do not post them publicly unless you give us written permission.

6. Cameras and recordings

Our Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy forbids hidden recording devices and sets rules for cameras in homes. Porchlight does not install cameras in your home or in any Sitter's home. We may keep photos and notes made under that policy for dispute handling.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account, pet and home information: while your account is active and for a reasonable time afterward.
  • Booking and payment records: as long as we need them for taxes, accounting and disputes, usually at least seven years.
  • Consent records: for as long as the agreement they relate to could matter, and for at least seven years.
  • Sitter application and background-check information: as the law requires and for a reasonable time after we close your file.
  • Access codes and keys: removed or returned when you stop using Porchlight or ask us to.

You can ask us to delete information, and we will delete what we are not required or permitted to keep.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted connections, private storage for records and photos, and access limited by role. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

You can view and update your pets, home and contact details in your portal. You may ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop using it for certain purposes, by emailing hello@porchlightpetcare.com. Residents of some states have additional privacy rights, and we will honor them where they apply. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

10. Children

Porchlight is for adults. You must be at least 18 to create an account or work as a Sitter. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Links to other sites

Our site may link to other sites (for example, a payment app, a map or a rescue partner). We are not responsible for their practices.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version with its date, and for a material change we will ask you to accept it before your next booking or login.
