This policy applies to owners and sitters. It is part of the Owner Service Agreement, the Sitter Agreement and the Terms of Service. Where this policy is stricter than something else you have agreed to, this policy applies.

## 1. Cameras and recording devices

We want pets cared for at home, and we want everyone in the home to be able to trust it. Our rule is simple.

1. **No hidden recording devices. Ever.** No camera, microphone or other recording device may be hidden, disguised, or placed where it cannot be seen. This applies to owners, sitters, and anyone else, in the owner's home and anywhere a sitter is working.
2. **All cameras must be disclosed before booking.** An owner who has a camera anywhere on the property (indoors, outdoors, doorbell, driveway, nanny or pet cam, or a smart display) must disclose each camera in the booking and say where it points.
3. **No cameras in private spaces.** No camera or recording device may be in, or pointed into, a bathroom, a bedroom, or any place a sitter sleeps, changes clothes or keeps personal belongings.
4. **No indoor audio.** Owners must turn off the microphone, audio recording, two-way talk and remote listening on every indoor camera and smart device for the whole visit or stay. Recording a conversation you are not part of can violate state and federal law.
5. **Outdoor and doorbell cameras** that are disclosed and visible are fine, as long as they record video only. Their microphones and remote listening must be turned off during care, and they must not look into a bathroom, bedroom, or other private space.
6. **Drop-ins, walks and check-ins:** disclosed, visible, video-only cameras in common areas are allowed. **Overnight stays:** all indoor cameras and other indoor surveillance must be off, unplugged or covered for the whole stay, and the owner says so in the booking.
7. **Sitters' devices.** A sitter may carry an ordinary phone or smartwatch without listing it. A sitter must disclose, and get the owner's permission in advance for, any device the sitter intends to use to monitor or record. Sitters do not record or photograph the inside of a home except the visit-report photos and the agreed condition photos of unoccupied areas, and do not post photos or video of a home, its contents, mail, documents, or an owner's pets or family on social media without the owner's written permission. Nothing in this section stops anyone from making an emergency call, reporting a crime or safety concern to the proper authority, or keeping lawfully obtained evidence.
8. A sitter who finds an undisclosed camera or recording device should leave it alone, step out of its view if possible, and tell Porchlight right away. A sitter may end the stay at once, with pay for the visit or night, and the owner is not entitled to a refund.
9. A violation of this section can lead to immediate removal from Porchlight and may also be a crime.

## 2. Home condition before a visit or stay (owner)

The owner agrees that, before every visit and stay:

- The home is reasonably clean and safe to enter and to stay in.
- All pet waste is cleaned up, litter boxes are scooped, and enough food, water, litter, waste bags, medication and supplies are on hand for the whole booking, plus a few extra days.
- The sleeping area is clean, with fresh bedding, for overnight stays, and the sitter can use a bathroom, the kitchen and a place to put personal belongings.
- Any hazard is disclosed in writing before the booking. Examples: a pool, a pond, unstable stairs, a broken gate or fence, a space heater, a pest problem (fleas, ticks, bedbugs, rodents), mold, smoke or strong odors, aggressive animals, a person who will be in the home, or anything else that could harm a person or an animal.
- Firearms, medications that are not for the pet, and valuables are secured and out of reach or sight.
- Alarm systems, locks and smart devices work, and the codes you gave us are correct.

A sitter may refuse to start, or may leave, a home that is unsafe, unsanitary or not as described. If the sitter has to do this because the home was not as described, the visit or night is billed in full.

## 3. Home condition after a visit or stay (sitter)

The sitter agrees that, before leaving at the end of each visit and at the end of the stay:

- The home is left as it was found, with doors and windows locked and the alarm set as the owner asked.
- Pet messes are cleaned up, food and water bowls are refilled and washed as needed, litter boxes are scooped, waste is taken out as the owner asked, and dishes the sitter used are washed.
- Anything the sitter used from the owner's own supplies beyond pet food and pet supplies is replaced or reported.
- Nothing is moved, borrowed or removed from the home. The sitter does not use the owner's belongings other than the kitchen, bathroom, bed and linens set aside for an overnight stay, and the television and Wi-Fi in common spaces with permission.
- The sitter does not enter rooms the owner has marked off-limits, does not have guests, and does not bring their own animals or children unless the owner has agreed in writing in the booking.

## 4. Condition photos and damage reports

- At the start and end of every overnight stay, the sitter takes timestamped photos of the main living areas, the sleeping area, the kitchen and bathroom, and the doors, and uploads them to the visit report. For shorter visits, the sitter photographs any existing damage or mess found on arrival.
- Anyone who finds damage, loss, or a mess should tell Porchlight in writing as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours of the end of the stay, with photos if they have them. Prompt notice helps us investigate. A loss found later can still be reported and will still be looked into, though older reports may be harder to confirm.
- Porchlight will ask both sides for their account and photos and will work toward a fair result. Ordinary wear and tear, and damage caused by an animal that the owner told us about and that was kept to the rules in the booking, are not a sitter's responsibility unless the sitter was careless.

## 5. Keys, codes and access

- Codes and keys are used only for the booking, are never shared, and are not written down with the owner's name or address.
- Owners are encouraged to change door and alarm codes after a stay.
- Keys are returned when requested and always at the end of the relationship.
- A sitter who loses a key or code tells Porchlight at once.

## 6. Safety

- Everyone follows the law, including leash laws and local pet limits.
- The sitter never leaves an animal unattended outside, in a car, or off-leash in an unfenced area unless the owner specifically allowed it in writing.
- If an animal is injured or ill, the sitter follows the owner's emergency plan and contacts the owner, then the owner's backup contact, then Porchlight. See the Owner Service Agreement for veterinary authority.
- A sitter may leave and end a visit or stay immediately, with pay, if there is violence, threats, harassment, illegal activity, intoxication, an unsafe animal, or any situation the sitter reasonably feels is unsafe. The sitter should call 911 if anyone is in danger and then call Porchlight.
- Neither owners nor sitters may be intoxicated, impaired, or under the influence of illegal drugs during a visit or stay, and sitters do not drink alcohol or use cannabis or other drugs while on duty.
- No smoking or vaping in an owner's home during a visit or stay unless the owner has allowed it in writing.

## 7. Respect

Owners and sitters treat each other with courtesy. Porchlight does not accept harassment or discrimination of any kind. A sitter may decline an animal or a home for safety reasons, but we will not allow a sitter to refuse a booking because of an owner's race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, age or any other protected status.

## 8. Reporting a problem

Report a problem to Porchlight at 470-424-3183 or hello@porchlightpetcare.com as soon as you can. In an emergency, call 911 first.
