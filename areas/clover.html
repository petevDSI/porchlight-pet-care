Pet care in Clover

The story people tell about Clover is a good one. In 1876 it was a railway stop between Yorkville and Gastonia, and the name supposedly came from a patch of clover that got watered by overflow from the railway tank. The town was incorporated in 1887, had several cotton mills for decades, and today has a downtown historic district on the National Register and a new batch of homes around it.

It is a small town that is getting bigger, up about a third since 2010, and it is a long enough drive from Charlotte that people who live here tend to work somewhere and be gone a lot. That is the kind of household that benefits from a dependable midday walk or a sitter who comes to the house when you are away for the weekend. Clover is on the western edge of our area, so we plan visits here in blocks to keep drive time sensible, and your booking will show which times are open.

The dog-walking is better than the town’s size suggests. Clover Community Park has shaded walking trails, a covered shelter and a nine-hole disc golf course, and the Battle of Kings Mountain site is a short drive west of town, where leashed dogs are welcome on the grounds.

Local walks

Where dogs go in Clover

Clover Community Park

On Community Park Dr. It has shaded walking trails, picnic tables, a covered shelter, playgrounds, athletic fields and a nine-hole disc golf course. Dogs are allowed, so check the posted leash rules.

Clover’s other town parks

New Centre Park, Roosevelt Park and Field Day Park are all in town, which gives you a different walk every day of the week.

Kings Mountain National Military Park

The site of the 1780 Battle of Kings Mountain, a short drive west of town. Leashed pets are welcome on the grounds on a leash no longer than six feet, but not in the visitor center.

Kings Mountain State Park

Close by, with more than 6,800 acres of hiking trails. Pets are allowed in most outdoor areas on a leash of six feet or less.
