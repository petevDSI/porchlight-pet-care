Questions

About working with Porchlight

How much do I earn?

You keep 88% of every booking and 100% of tips. Our listed prices start at $30 for a 30-minute walk or visit, so a $30 booking pays you $26.40. Overnight stays and other services pay on the same split.

Do I need experience?

You need to be comfortable and reliable with animals and with being in other people’s homes. Experience with your own pets counts. Tell us what you’ve done and we’ll talk through the rest.

Am I an employee?

No. You work as an independent contractor. You choose which requests to accept, you set your own availability, and you’re responsible for your own taxes. We report your earnings as the IRS requires.

How do I get paid?

Clients pay Porchlight when they book. We pay you for the visits you complete, and we’ll explain the schedule when you start.

What do you check before I start?

A background check, a conversation, and references. Every client meets their sitter first, so you’ll be introduced at a free meet-and-greet.

Do I need my own insurance?

Ask us in your conversation. We’ll explain what is covered and what we expect before your first booking.

Can I keep working with my own clients or on another platform?

Yes. You’re independent. If you use another platform, follow its rules about bookings that started there, and keep Porchlight bookings with Porchlight.

Which towns do you serve?

Belmont, Mt. Holly, Cramerton, McAdenville, Lowell, Ranlo and East Gastonia in North Carolina, and Lake Wylie, Clover, York, India Hook and Newport in South Carolina, west of the Catawba River.

How soon will I hear back?

We read every application and reply within a few days to set up a conversation.
