Mt. Holly, NC

Dog walking and pet sitting in Mt. Holly, NC.

Mt. Holly is a river town with a real dog-walking map: a fenced dog park, a paved greenway beside the Catawba, and a historic downtown. We walk it, and we sit for the pets who live here.

Pet care in Mt. Holly

Mt. Holly grew up around cotton. The Mount Holly Cotton Mill opened in 1875, gave the town its name, and is still the oldest surviving mill in Gaston County. Today the mill and the downtown historic district are both on the National Register, and the town has kept its small-city feel while it has grown by almost a third since 2010.

For dog people, the best thing about Mt. Holly is that the walking infrastructure is already here. Tuckaseege Park has a divided off-leash dog park for small and large breeds, walking trails through the surrounding wetlands, and the start of a paved greenway that follows the Catawba River north. A dog who gets a proper outing here is a calmer dog on the couch afterward.

We use the park the way a good sitter should: leash on outside the fenced area, one dog at a time unless you tell us your dogs are friends, and always a bag in the pocket. If your dog loves the river but is not a strong recall dog, we stick to the greenway and keep the long leash on.

Local walks

Where we walk in Mt. Holly

Tuckaseege Park

At 231 Broome St. It has a fenced dog park divided for small and large dogs, a playground, athletic fields, restrooms and a pavilion. Dogs must be leashed when they leave the dog park.

Catawba Riverfront Greenway

A paved 1.4-mile trail that begins at Tuckaseege Park and follows the Catawba River north. At the end it connects to the Mount Holly Linear Park Trail, which adds another 0.3 miles.

Wetland trails at Tuckaseege

Shorter natural-surface trails connect to the park, good for a sniffy walk when the paved greenway feels too busy.

Downtown Mt. Holly

The historic district has restaurants, a coffee shop and shaded sidewalks. A nice short loop for older dogs.

About pet care in Mt. Holly

Can you take my dog to the Tuckaseege Park dog park?

Yes, if your dog is social and vaccinated. The park has separate small-dog and large-dog sides. We always ask at the meet-and-greet whether your dog does well with others, and we only use the off-leash area if the answer is a clear yes.

How far is Mt. Holly from your Belmont home base?

It is one of the closest towns to Belmont, so Mt. Holly visits are some of the easiest to schedule, including midday walks.

Do you sit for pets while we travel?

Yes. Overnight stays, drop-in visits for cats and small pets, and home check-ins for plants and mail are all available in Mt. Holly. Every client starts with a free meet-and-greet at your home.
