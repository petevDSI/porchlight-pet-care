Hour by hour

How a pet taxi ride goes

  1. Pickup

    Hello and secure

    A calm greeting, leash and harness or carrier checked, and your appointment details confirmed.

  2. Ride

    Straight there

    The direct route, with the heat or air on and your pet buckled in or crated.

  3. Arrive

    Hand-off

    We check your pet in with the clinic or groomer and leave your phone number.

  4. Return

    Pick up again

    On a round trip, we collect your pet when they’re ready and bring them home.

  5. Home

    Safe and settled

    Water, a quick potty break, and a text letting you know they’re back.

Times are a guide. We follow your pet’s routine, not ours.

What we need from you

Put these in the booking notes and the ride goes smoothly.

  • The destination addressThe vet, groomer or boarding facility, and the appointment time.
  • A carrier, or a harness that fitsCats and small pets travel in a carrier. Dogs in a crate or a harness we can clip in.
  • A heads-up to the clinicTell them Porchlight is dropping off. Only you, or a contact you list, can approve treatment.
  • Your pet’s paperworkVaccine records or forms the clinic needs, in a folder or an envelope.
  • A phone that’s onThe vet may need to reach you, and so may we.

Questions

About pet taxi

Which pets can ride?

Dogs, cats and small pets in a carrier. Tell us the breed and weight when you book so we can confirm they ride safely in our vehicle.

Do you stay with my pet at the vet?

We check your pet in and hand them to the staff. On a round trip we come back for them when they’re ready. If we wait with your pet, the first 10 minutes are free and then it’s $10 for each 15 minutes. We don’t make medical decisions: you, or a contact you list, approve treatment.

Who pays the vet or groomer?

You do, directly. We bring your pet and bring them home; we don’t pay for or collect for the clinic.

What if the appointment runs long?

Text us. A short delay is part of a round trip. For long waits we’ll agree on the extra cost with you first.

Can I book the same ride every week?

Yes. Weekly rides to a groomer, daycare or physical therapy can be booked as a repeating request.
