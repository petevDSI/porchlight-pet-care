This is a stand-alone notice. It contains only this disclosure and your authorization.

## Disclosure

Porchlight Pet & Home Care ("Porchlight") may obtain a consumer report and/or investigative consumer report about you from a consumer reporting agency, for the purpose of deciding whether to approve you to work as an independent pet sitter. The report may include your identity verification, Social Security number trace, criminal-record searches (county, state, federal, and national), sex-offender registry search, and, if you will drive animals, your driving record. Porchlight may also obtain a report during your time with Porchlight to review whether you may continue.

## Your authorization

By checking the box, you authorize Porchlight and the consumer reporting agency it uses to obtain these reports about you, and you confirm that the information you gave on your application, including your full legal name and date of birth, is accurate.

## Your rights

- If Porchlight is considering not approving you, or removing you, based in whole or in part on a report, we will first send you a copy of the report and a summary of your rights under the Fair Credit Reporting Act, and give you a reasonable chance to respond.
- If we then decide not to move forward, we will tell you, give you the name and contact information of the agency, and explain that the agency did not make the decision and cannot explain why it was made. You have the right to a free copy of the report from the agency within 60 days and to dispute its accuracy or completeness directly with the agency.
- You may ask whether a report was obtained and for the name of the agency by contacting hello@porchlightpetcare.com.

## Keeping it private

Report information is used only to decide whether you may work with Porchlight and to keep animals and owners safe. It is kept confidential and is available only to the people at Porchlight who need it.
