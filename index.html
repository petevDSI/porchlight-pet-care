    Questions

    What people ask before the first visit.

    Do we meet you before you have a key?

    Always. The first step is a free meet-and-greet at your home, so your pets know us and we know their routine before any visit is booked.

    How do you get into the house?

    A lockbox or a door code is easiest. If you'd rather hand over a key, it's tagged with a code, never your name or address.

    Are you insured and bonded?

    Yes. We carry pet-care liability insurance and a bond, and we're glad to show proof at the meet-and-greet.

    Will I hear from you during a visit?

    After every visit you get a short note and a photo. On overnights, one in the evening and one in the morning.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Cancel at least 24 hours ahead and there's no charge. Later than that, the visit is billed.

    What happens if my pet gets sick while I'm away?

    We call you first, then your vet. If we can't reach either, we go to the emergency clinic you named at the meet-and-greet.

    What if it's storming?

    We still come. In heavy rain or extreme heat the walk gets shorter and the indoor play gets longer, and we'll tell you so in the visit note.

    Heading out of town? We'll leave the light on.
