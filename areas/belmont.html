Pet care in Belmont

Belmont is a town of two eras sitting on top of each other. The old one is a mill town: the railroad stop that became Belmont in 1883 grew into a textile center, and the mills shaped the town for the next century. The new one is a commuter town that has grown by about half in ten years, with people who drive to Charlotte for work and come home to a dog that has been waiting.

That second group is who this page is for. If you leave at 7:30 and get back at 6:00, a dog that is home for ten hours needs a break in the middle of the day, and a cat or a senior pet may need eyes on them. A midday walk or a drop-in from someone who already knows the house and the routine fixes that. And because Belmont is home base, we can usually offer the widest choice of times here, including the early and late ones.

The town is easy to walk. Downtown Belmont is a National Register historic district with Tudor Revival, Craftsman and Colonial Revival buildings, and the streets around Belmont Abbey, founded by Benedictine monks in 1876, make a good loop for a dog who likes to see things. Water is never far away either, since the town is nearly surrounded by the Catawba and the arms of Lake Wylie.

Local walks

Around Belmont with a dog

Downtown historic district

Flat sidewalks, shade in places, and plenty to sniff. It is the walk we do most when a dog wants a neighborhood loop with some variety.

Goat Island and the Cramerton greenway

A short drive away, Goat Island in Cramerton is a shady spot for dogs and owners, with a greenway that leads to downtown Cramerton. It is a favorite for a longer weekend outing.

Dog-friendly patios

The Station at South Main Cycles, a converted train depot with a patio, and the covered patio at The String Bean are both listed as dog-friendly by Go Gaston. Handy after a walk.

Belmont Abbey grounds

The abbey and college campus is a National Register historic district. It is one of the prettiest places in town to walk, so check campus rules for pets before you go.

About pet care in Belmont

Is Belmont really your home base?

Yes. Porchlight is run from Belmont, NC, so travel time to a Belmont home is the shortest of any town we serve. That is why we can usually offer more start times here than anywhere else.

I commute to Charlotte. Can you cover my work day?

That is the most common reason people call us. Charlotte is about 12 miles east, and a typical commute means a dog home alone for nine or ten hours. A midday walk or drop-in splits that day in half. You can book it as a one-off or every weekday.

Do you also take pets to the vet from Belmont?

Yes. Our pet taxi picks up at your Belmont home and drives your pet to a vet, groomer or anywhere else they need to go. It is priced by the mile, and you see the fare before you book.
