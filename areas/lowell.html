Pet care in Lowell

Lowell took its name from Lowell, Massachusetts, in the hope it would become a textile center like it. It started as a railroad depot called Wright’s Station, after the nearest resident, and grew around the Woodlawn Mill, built on the South Fork in 1848. It was incorporated in 1879, and the Lowell Teacherage, built in 1924 to house teachers, is a Gaston County historic property.

Lowell is one of the steadier towns in the area, with about 3,650 people, up slightly since 2010. That means settled households, many with older dogs, and a lot of clients who need dependable care more than novelty: the same walker, the same time, the same keys. That is how we run a Lowell route.

The big local draw is George Poston Park, northwest of town on Lowell-Spencer Mountain Road. It is run by Gaston County and has playgrounds, ball fields and a mountain bike trail, plus a five-acre fenced dog park split into sections for dogs under 25 pounds and larger dogs.

Local walks

Around Lowell with a dog

George Poston Park dog park

A fenced, off-leash, five-acre park with separate areas for small and large dogs. Waste bags are provided. We use it with dogs who are social, vaccinated and have good recall.

George Poston Park trails and fields

The park also has a mountain bike trail and open fields for a leashed walk. Dogs should be leashed outside the fenced dog park.

South Fork river corridor

The river borders the city on the north. The South Fork Catawba is a flatwater paddling route and a scenic backdrop for walks.

Downtown Lowell streets

Quiet streets and older homes make an easy loop for a senior dog.

About pet care in Lowell

Do you take dogs to the George Poston dog park from Lowell?

Yes, with your permission and only for dogs who do well with others. The park has a small-dog area for dogs under 25 pounds and a separate area for larger dogs.

I have an older dog who needs short, gentle walks. Is that okay?

It is one of the most common requests. A 30-minute walk can be a slow neighborhood stroll, and we follow your dog’s pace, not a schedule.

Is Lowell inside your service area?

Yes. Lowell is east of Gastonia in Gaston County, and the booking form checks your exact address when you book.
