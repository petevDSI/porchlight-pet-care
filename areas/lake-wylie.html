Lake Wylie, SC

Pet sitting and dog walking in Lake Wylie, SC.

Lake Wylie is a lakeside community full of busy families, boat people and dogs who think a dock is a trampoline. We look after pets in lake homes with that in mind.

Pet care in Lake Wylie

Lake Wylie has changed fast. The community grew from about 8,800 people in 2010 to about 13,700 in 2020. Fast growth means busy roads, which is one more reason to have a sitter who plans around traffic and can reach your house when you cannot.

Pets who live on or near the lake have their own risks. Docks, seawalls and steep banks are hard on a puppy or an older dog, and a dog who swims too long in warm weather can get into trouble. When we sit in a lake home we ask about the water on the first visit: where the dog can safely go in, where they must not, and whether they wear a life vest on the boat. Then we follow your rule.

It also helps that we are not a national app. Lake Wylie is one of the towns we plan around, a meet-and-greet at your home is free. If you leave for the long weekend, we come to the house, not the other way around.

About pet care in Lake Wylie

Do you work with dogs who swim?

Yes, with your rules. Tell us at the meet-and-greet where your dog may go in, where they may not, and whether they should be towel-dried and rinsed after a lake swim. We write it into your pet profile so every visit follows it.

Can you do overnight stays at a lake house?

Yes. Overnight pet sitting means we stay in your home, so your pets keep their routine. It is a good fit for lake owners who travel for work or for a long weekend.

Which side of the lake do you cover?

We cover the west side of the Catawba River and Lake Wylie, including the Lake Wylie community. If you are on the east side near Tega Cay or Fort Mill, you are just outside our area, but you can request a quote and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you.
