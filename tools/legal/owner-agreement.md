This agreement is between you (the "Owner") and Porchlight Pet & Home Care, a business based in Belmont, North Carolina ("Porchlight," "we," "us"). It covers pet care you book through Porchlight with an independent sitter. It works together with the Terms of Service, the Animals in the Home Policy, the Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy, and, for overnight stays, the Overnight Stay Addendum.

## 1. What Porchlight is and is not

Porchlight runs a neighborhood network of independent pet sitters and handles scheduling, payments, vetting, and visit reports. **Sitters are independent contractors, not Porchlight employees.** Your booking confirmation names the sitter who is the care provider for that booking, and Porchlight handles payment, refunds and support. Porchlight screens sitters (see "How we vet sitters" on our site) but does not supervise them in your home and does not guarantee any particular outcome.

## 2. Meet-and-greet first

Visits are confirmed only after a free meet-and-greet at your home with your sitter, so the sitter and your animals know each other. You may decline a sitter after meeting them and Porchlight will try to offer another.

## 3. What you promise about your animals

You promise that:

- You are the owner of the animals, or you have permission from the owner to book care for them.
- You have told us about **every animal in the home** as required by the Animals in the Home Policy, and you understand each additional animal is charged for each night of an overnight stay, and on walks, drop-ins and check-ins when it needs care from us. Fish and reptiles that need no care are not charged.
- You have given us accurate information about each animal's health, medications, diet, behavior, bite or aggression history, escape habits, fears, and anything the sitter needs to know to be safe.
- Dogs, cats and ferrets are vaccinated against rabies as North Carolina and South Carolina law requires, and you will provide the current certificate and its expiration date. A booking may be paused until we have it.
- You will tell us right away if an animal's health, behavior or the number of animals in the home changes.
- You will keep your animals' tags, microchip information and your contact information current.

You understand that a sitter may decline or end care of an animal that is unsafe.

## 4. What you promise about your home

You promise that:

- You have the right to give us access to the home, and you have told us about any landlord, HOA, or household rules that affect a sitter's presence or an overnight stay.
- The home meets the standards in the Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy before each visit or stay, and you will disclose hazards, pests, other people in the home, and anything that could affect safety.
- **There are no hidden cameras or recording devices.** You have disclosed every camera on the property and where it points. For overnight stays, all indoor cameras and other indoor surveillance are off, unplugged or covered, and indoor audio recording is off. Disclosed outdoor and doorbell cameras are video-only, with their microphones and remote listening turned off during care.
- The access codes, keys, alarm information and Wi-Fi information you provide are correct, and you will tell us if they change.
- You are reachable, or your backup contact is, during the booking.

## 5. Visit reports and photos

Your sitter will send a visit report, which may include photos or short videos of your animals. Porchlight may use the visit-report photo of your animals on our site or social media only if you give us your written permission. We do not post photos of the inside of your home or your family without your written permission.

## 6. Emergency veterinary care

If an animal needs urgent care and the sitter cannot reach you or your backup contact, you authorize Porchlight and the sitter to take the animal to your veterinarian or your named emergency clinic, and to approve treatment up to the amount you listed on your client form, at your expense. If you did not list an amount, you authorize the care that is reasonably necessary to relieve suffering or save the animal's life, up to $500. You will pay the veterinarian directly and will repay Porchlight or the sitter anything they pay on your behalf.

If an animal dies or is lost while in the sitter's care, the sitter will tell you and Porchlight immediately and will follow the instructions you gave us for that situation.

## 7. Payment, cancellation and charges

- You pay up front when you book at the published prices, plus any additional-animal charge and any holiday charge, all shown before you pay. A tip is optional.
- Cancel at least 24 hours before a visit or stay for a full refund. Later cancellations are billed in full.
- If the animals or the home turn out to be different from what you told us (for example, an animal you did not list), we may invoice the additional-animal rate shown when you booked for each visit or night we can substantiate. The invoice shows the dates, the rate, the basis and how to dispute it.
- Taxi waiting time and other published charges that are billed afterward are due when billed.

## 8. Your responsibility for your animals

You remain legally responsible for your animals. This includes injury or damage they cause to a sitter, another person, another animal or property, unless it was caused by the sitter's negligence. If your animal bites or injures someone, you will tell us right away. We recommend that you confirm that your homeowner's or renter's insurance covers injury or damage caused by your animals.

## 9. Our responsibility

Porchlight and your sitter will use reasonable care. Our responsibility to you is described in the Terms of Service. Nothing in this agreement limits a claim that cannot be limited by law.

## 10. Acknowledgment of risks and limited release

Pets can become ill, injured, anxious or lost despite careful care, and older and sick animals and certain breeds carry added risk. You accept those inherent risks, meaning risks that can occur even when reasonable care is used. To the fullest extent allowed by law, you (for yourself and anyone who might claim through you) release Porchlight, its owners, and its sitters from claims for loss of, injury to, or illness or death of an animal, or damage to property, that result from those inherent risks.

You are **not** releasing:

- A claim that Porchlight or the sitter was negligent or did not do what they promised in this agreement, including reasonable direct losses such as emergency veterinary bills, costs to find and recover a lost animal, repair of damaged property, and rekeying or changing access after lost or misused keys or codes.
- Any claim for injury to you.

The release, liability cap, and damages exclusions in these documents do not apply to fraud, gross negligence, willful or intentional misconduct, bodily injury to a person, or any liability or remedy that applicable law protects from limitation.

## 11. Your promise to cover claims you cause

You agree to cover Porchlight and the sitter for third-party losses and costs (including reasonable attorney's fees) to the extent they arise because you gave us false or incomplete information about your animals, your home or the people in it, or because of your animals' behavior. This does not cover losses to the extent they are caused by the negligence or misconduct of the sitter or Porchlight.

## 12. Privacy

The information you give us (including your address, codes, and animal records) is used as described in our Privacy Policy. Codes and home details are visible only to you, to Porchlight staff who need them, and to the sitter assigned to a confirmed visit.

## 13. Changes and ending

We may update this agreement. If we make a material change, we will ask you to accept the new version before your next booking. You may stop using Porchlight at any time. Sections that are meant to continue (payment, risk acknowledgment, cover-claims, and dispute terms) continue after the relationship ends.

## 14. Governing law

North Carolina law applies, mandatory protections that apply to you remain available, and disputes are handled as the Terms of Service describe, including a South Carolina court for bookings in South Carolina.

## 15. Your acceptance

By checking the box next to this agreement, you confirm that you have read it, that you are an adult who can enter into it, and that you agree to it. We record the date, time, and version of what you accepted.
