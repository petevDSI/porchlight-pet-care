Pet care in McAdenville

McAdenville was incorporated in 1881 and named for Rufus Yancey McAden, whose mill anchored the town. Pharr Yarns, founded here in 1939, was the major employer for most of a century, and the McAdenville Historic District has been on the National Register since 2009. The town is small, under a thousand residents, so a pet-care company that knows the roads and neighbors matters.

The tradition everyone knows is Christmas Town USA. The lights began in 1956, with help from Pharr Yarns, and today the display has 375 lit trees and more than 200 wreaths on the light poles. The town reports more than 600,000 vehicles a year. If you live here, that is a lot of cars on your street in December. We plan evening visits with that traffic in mind, so a drop-in or a walk is not left to the last minute.

The rest of the year it is a peaceful place to walk. McAdenville Lake sits in the middle of town behind a dam on the South Fork, and the river itself is a paddling route: Go Gaston suggests putting in below the McAdenville dam for a slow, flat float with a dog.

McAdenville Lake

The lake sits in the center of town, formed by a dam on the South Fork. It gives the town a natural loop for quiet, shaded walks.

South Fork river float

Go Gaston recommends putting in below the McAdenville dam, or in Cramerton, for a flatwater paddle with your dog. A life vest is a good idea for any dog who goes on the water.

Historic district streets

The mill-village streets are on the National Register and make a calm walk outside the lights season.
