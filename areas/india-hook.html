Pet care in India Hook

India Hook is a census-designated place in York County, on the shore of Lake Wylie, and most of it is now part of the city of Rock Hill. It is one of the fastest-growing spots in our area: the population went from about 1,600 in 2000 to about 2,050 in 2010 and to nearly 3,800 in 2020.

It sits at the eastern edge of our service area, which runs west of the Catawba River and Lake Wylie. That is a real border, not a marketing one, and it is why we ask for your street address at booking. Some India Hook addresses are inside, some are just outside, and the form tells you right away. If yours is outside, you can request a quote and we will tell you honestly what is possible.

For lake households the care questions are practical ones: where the dog may go into the water, whether they are comfortable on a dock, and what the plan is if a storm rolls in while you are away. We ask at the meet-and-greet, write the answers into your pet’s profile, and follow them every visit.

About pet care in India Hook

Is India Hook inside your service area?

Some of it is. Our border follows the western side of the Catawba River and Lake Wylie, and India Hook is right at the edge. Enter your street address in the booking form and it will tell you immediately. If you are just outside, request a quote.

Do you do overnight stays at a lake house?

Yes. We stay in the home, so your pets keep their routine, and we follow your water-safety rules for the dock and the shore.

Can your pet taxi go beyond my town?

Yes. We pick up inside our area and drive anywhere you need to go, with a fare shown before you book.
