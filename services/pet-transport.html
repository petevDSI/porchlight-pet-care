How a long-distance trip goes

  1. Request

    Tell us the route

    Where from, where to, when, and who is traveling. We reply with a written quote.

  2. Before

    Records and a plan

    We confirm the health papers your route needs and agree on pickup and drop-off times.

  3. Pickup

    Meet and secure

    Hand-off

    Your pet goes only to the person you named, and you get a photo and a text.

Times are a guide. We follow your pet’s routine, not ours.

What you’ll need to have ready

We’ll send a checklist with your quote. The common items:

  • The route and datesStart and end addresses, a pickup window, and how flexible you are.
  • Your pet’s detailsSpecies, breed, age and weight, and anything that affects a long ride, like motion sickness or anxiety.
  • Health recordsCurrent vaccinations including rabies. Many interstate trips also need a Certificate of Veterinary Inspection.
  • A crate or harness that fitsWe can use yours, so the crate smells like home.
  • Food, medication and a comfort itemEnough for the trip, plus a day extra in case of delays.
  • Someone to meet your petA named person with a phone number at the destination.

How the price works

Every trip is different, so each one is quoted.

  • A written quote up frontBased on distance, the time on the road and tolls, so you know the cost before you commit.
  • Longer trips may include an overnightIf a trip can’t be driven in a day, the quote will include the extra night.
  • More than one pet?Pets from the same household can travel together, which usually costs less than separate trips.

Questions

About pet transport

How far will you drive?

We’ll quote any trip we can drive safely and reliably. Longer routes are quoted with an overnight stop.

Can you bring home a puppy from a breeder or rescue?

Yes. Puppies need to be old enough and vaccinated for travel under the rules of the states involved, and we’ll ask for their records before the trip.

Will my pet ride with other clients’ animals?

Not unless you agree to it. Pets from the same household travel together.

What health papers do I need?

It depends on the route. For many interstate trips, a Certificate of Veterinary Inspection from a licensed vet is required, plus current rabies vaccination. We’ll tell you what applies when we quote.

How do I get a price?

Send a quote request with the route and dates. We’ll reply with a written price, usually within a day.
