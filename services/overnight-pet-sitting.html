What’s included

Every overnight includes

Evening and morning walks

A proper walk at dusk and again first thing, at your dog’s pace and on their usual route.

Dinner and breakfast

Meals measured exactly as you leave them, fresh water topped up, bowls washed.

Playtime

Fetch in the yard, tug, puzzle toys, or a good brush, whatever your pet loves.

Couch time and a movie

An evening on the sofa together, TV on low, so the house feels like a normal night.

Snuggles on request

Lap dogs get a lap. Cats who like their space get their space.

A bedtime routine

Last potty break, the treat they always get, their blanket, a story read aloud if that’s what they’re used to. We follow your routine.

Medications on schedule

Pills, drops and injections given exactly when you say, logged so you can see it happened.

Home care included

Mail and packages in, trash out on trash night, lights on and off so the house looks lived in.

Evening and morning updates

A note and photos at night, another in the morning, in your client portal and by text.

What your sitter needs from you

Set these out before you leave and the night runs smoothly.

  • A clean bed with fresh sheetsA guest room or made-up couch is fine. Let us know which.
  • Access to a bathroom and showerPlus a clean towel, so your sitter can get ready in the morning.
  • Enough food, treats and meds for the whole stayWith a day or two extra in case your flight is delayed.
  • Wi-Fi name and passwordSo updates and photos reach you without a hitch.
  • Your pet’s routine, written downMeal times, amounts, bedtime ritual, where they sleep. The client portal has a place for all of it.
  • A way in and a spare keyLockbox or door code, plus a backup in case a battery dies.
  • Your vet and an emergency contactAlready on file if you’ve filled out the new client form.

What your sitter brings

You don’t need to supply any of this.

  • Their own toiletriesAnd a change of clothes. No need to stock anything extra.
  • Poop bags and a pet first-aid kitAlways in the car, always restocked.
  • A phone charger and a calm presencePlus a strict no-guests, no-parties rule. It’s your home.

Questions

About overnight stays

How many hours is an overnight?

Your sitter arrives around 6:30 pm and leaves around 8:00 am, roughly 13 hours. If your pets need a different window, tell us and we’ll match it.

Is my pet left alone during the day?

An overnight covers evening to morning. For dogs who can’t be alone all day, add a midday walk or drop-in while you’re away.

Will the sitter have guests over?

Never. No guests, no parties, no other pets brought along.

Can you sleep where my dog sleeps?

Yes. If your dog sleeps in your bedroom, tell us whether you’d like your sitter there, on the couch nearby, or in the guest room.

How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks for regular dates, and earlier for holidays, which fill first.
