Legal

These are the agreements and policies that apply when you use Porchlight. Each one is versioned, and we record which version you accepted.

  • Animals in the Home Policy
    Every animal in the home is disclosed and counted, whether or not it is the one you booked for. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Background Check Disclosure and Authorization
    Stand-alone notice and permission for the background check required before you work with Porchlight clients. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy
    Home condition before and after a stay, no hidden cameras, privacy, safety and damage reports. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Overnight Stay Addendum
    The clear-cut rules for any stay where a sitter sleeps at the owner's home. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Owner Service Agreement and Release
    What owners promise about their animals and home, what Porchlight and sitters provide, and the release. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Privacy Policy
    What information we collect, why, who sees it, and your choices. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Independent Sitter Agreement
    The independent-contractor terms for sitters working through Porchlight, including conduct, insurance, pay and removal. (version 2026-11-13)
  • Terms of Service
    The rules for using the Porchlight website, portals and booking system. (version 2026-11-13)
