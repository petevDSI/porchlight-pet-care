Meet your sitter

Hi, I’m Kraig.

I started Porchlight because leaving a pet behind shouldn’t feel like a gamble. When you’re away, I’m the one at your door: same face every time, someone you’ve met in person, who treats your home and your animals the way you would.

Why Porchlight

Someone home when you can’t be.

I grew up in a family that runs its own businesses, so I learned early that you build a reputation one promise at a time. Before Porchlight I worked in banking, where the whole job was getting the details exactly right and being someone people trusted with what mattered to them. Pet care turns out to be the same job.

The name comes from the porch light you leave on when someone’s coming home. That’s what I want Porchlight to feel like: the house isn’t empty, the dog got walked, the cat got her medicine on time, and you get a photo to prove it.

I live right here in Belmont, so I’m minutes away, not driving in from across Charlotte. I’m small on purpose. When Porchlight grows, every new sitter will be someone I’d trust with my own pets, and you’ll always meet them first.

My first dog was Shamus. When I left for college he stayed behind with my mom and Pete, and he became as much theirs as mine. He was part of every family hike, and he even shared the house with Pepper, my grandmother’s cat, without too much drama. Shamus is gone now, but he’s a big part of why I do this.
