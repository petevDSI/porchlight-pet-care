Questions

About drop-in visits

How many visits a day does my cat need?

Most cats do well with one visit a day. Twice a day is better for kittens, seniors, cats on medication, or trips longer than a few days.

Can you give insulin or other injections?

Yes. We’ll go over dose, timing and storage at the meet-and-greet.

What if my cat hides the whole time?

That’s normal. We still confirm they’re eating, drinking and using the box, and we’ll find them to lay eyes on them.

Do you care for pets other than cats and dogs?

Yes: rabbits, guinea pigs, birds, fish and reptiles.
