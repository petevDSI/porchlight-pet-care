Pet care in East Gastonia

East Gastonia is not a separate town. It is how people describe the east side of Gastonia, the part of the city that runs toward Cramerton and Belmont. Gastonia itself was incorporated in 1877 and named for jurist William Gaston, grew up around textile mills, and is now the biggest city in the county, with about 80,000 residents.

For pet owners that size is a plus. There are plenty of homes, plenty of commuters, and more parks than most people know about. We cover the east side of the city on the same terms as our other towns: a free meet-and-greet first, then walks, drop-ins and overnight stays, with your address checked against our service border at booking.

If your dog needs more than the block, the options are good. Rankin Lake Park has a paved loop around an 80-acre lake, George Poston Park has a fenced, off-leash dog park, and Crowders Mountain State Park is a bigger outing for a dog who loves a climb.

Local walks

Where dogs go near East Gastonia

Rankin Lake Park

At 1750 Rankin Lake Rd. A paved walking trail circles an 80-acre lake, with a fishing pier and picnic shelters. The park’s page does not state its dog rules, so keep your dog leashed and ask the park.

George Poston Park dog park

A five-acre, fenced, off-leash dog park with separate areas for dogs under 25 pounds and for larger dogs, run by Gaston County.

Crowders Mountain State Park

Trails up to the highest point in the county, west of the city. Check the park’s current rules for dogs and keep them leashed.

Downtown Gastonia

The Downtown Gastonia Historic District is on the National Register, and its streets make a good city walk for a dog who likes people-watching.

About pet care in East Gastonia

Where exactly do you cover in Gastonia?

We use the street address you enter at booking and compare it with our service border and drive time. The east side of the city is inside the area, and the booking form tells you right away if your address is not.

Can you take my dog to the George Poston Park dog park?

Yes, if your dog is social and up to date on vaccines. The park has a small-dog side under 25 pounds and a large-dog side. We confirm at the meet-and-greet that your dog does well with others first.

I live farther west in Gastonia. Can you still help?

Possibly. Request a quote and tell us your address and what you need, and we will tell you honestly whether we can reach you.
