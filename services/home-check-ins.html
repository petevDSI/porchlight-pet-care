How a check-in goes

  1. Arrive

    Outside first

    A look at doors, windows and the yard. Packages and mail collected.

  2. 10 min

    Inside walk-through

    Every room, the water heater, under sinks, the thermostat.

  3. 10 min

    Plants and lights

  4. 5 min

Questions

About home check-ins

How often should someone check on my house?

Every two to three days for most trips. Daily if you get lots of packages or have plants that need it.

What happens if you find a problem?

We call you right away, and with your permission call the contractor you listed. We send photos either way.

Do I need pets to book this?

No. Plenty of clients book check-ins for an empty house.

Can you start my car or run the faucets?

Yes. Small upkeep jobs like those are included; just tell us what you’d like done.
