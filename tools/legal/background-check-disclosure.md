This notice contains only this disclosure and your authorization.

## Disclosure

Porchlight Pet & Home Care ("Porchlight") may obtain a consumer report about you from a consumer reporting agency, for the purpose of deciding whether to approve you to work as an independent pet sitter. The report may include your identity verification, Social Security number trace, criminal-record searches (county, state, federal, and national), sex-offender registry search, and, if you will drive animals, your driving record. Porchlight may also obtain a report during your time with Porchlight to review whether you may continue.

## Your authorization

By checking the box, you authorize Porchlight and the consumer reporting agency it uses to obtain these reports about you.

Questions about this disclosure or your report: support@porchlightpetcare.com.
