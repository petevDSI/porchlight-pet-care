This addendum applies to every overnight stay and is part of the Owner Service Agreement, the Sitter Agreement and the Terms of Service. If it conflicts with them, this addendum controls for overnight stays.

## 1. What an overnight stay is

An overnight stay means a Porchlight sitter sleeps at the **owner's home** for the night to care for the animals there. It runs from the arrival time in the booking (normally evening) to the departure time in the booking (normally morning). Boarding an animal in the sitter's home is **not** part of this addendum and is not offered unless Porchlight approves it in writing for a specific booking.

## 2. Who may be in the home

- Only the sitter named in the booking may stay overnight. No other person may stay, visit, or sleep over.
- The sitter does not bring guests, partners, children, friends, or the sitter's own animals, unless the owner agreed in writing in the booking.
- The owner is responsible for telling us, in the booking, about **every person** who will be in the home during the stay (roommates, relatives, contractors, cleaners, house guests) and about every animal (see the Animals in the Home Policy).
- No parties, gatherings, or events.

## 3. Hours and duties

- The sitter arrives at the booked time, completes a walk-through with condition photos (see the Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy), and stays in the home overnight.
- The sitter does not leave the home for more than the times in the booking (for example, a walk or a short errand for the animals). A sitter who must leave for longer than that tells the owner and Porchlight first.
- The sitter does the feeding, water, walks, medications, and companionship listed in the owner's pet profile, and sends a visit report.
- If the sitter cannot complete a stay (illness, emergency, or safety), the sitter tells Porchlight at once. Porchlight will try to find a replacement and will refund any part of the stay that is not completed.

## 4. Sleeping and use of the home

- The owner names the room or bed where the sitter will sleep, and provides clean linens and access to a bathroom and the kitchen. If none is provided, the sitter may sleep on a couch.
- The sitter may use the kitchen, bathroom, television, and Wi-Fi in a reasonable way, as the owner permits. The sitter does not use other belongings and does not enter rooms marked off-limits.
- The sitter does not smoke, vape, or drink alcohol or use drugs in the home.

## 5. Cameras and recording

The rules in the Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy apply fully, and for overnight stays:

- Any indoor camera that can see the sleeping area, a bathroom, or a place the sitter changes clothes must be off, unplugged or covered for the whole stay.
- Indoor microphones and audio recording are off for the whole stay.
- The owner confirms at booking that no hidden camera or recording device is in the home and lists every disclosed camera and where it points.

## 6. Condition of the home

- The owner leaves the home clean and ready, with the sleeping area made up, supplies stocked, and waste cleaned up.
- The sitter leaves the home in the condition it was found, as described in the Home Standards, Cameras and Safety Policy.
- Both sides use the start-of-stay and end-of-stay photos to settle any disagreement.

## 7. Emergencies

- The owner gives us a phone number that will be answered or monitored during the stay, and a backup contact.
- In a medical emergency for an animal, the sitter will contact the owner, then the backup contact, then Porchlight, and then follow the veterinary authority in the Owner Service Agreement.
- If anyone in the home is in danger, the sitter will call 911.
- If the home becomes unsafe (fire, flood, break-in, power or heat failure, severe weather, or a safety threat), the sitter may leave with the animals only if it is safe and lawful, and will tell the owner and Porchlight at once.

## 8. Early return, late return, and changes

- If the owner returns early or the plans change, the owner tells us right away. An early return does not reduce the price for the booked night.
- If the owner's return is later than booked, the extra time is billed at the published rate for an additional night or part of a night, and the owner must tell us as soon as possible.
- The owner is responsible for being reachable. If the owner cannot be reached and the booking ends, the owner remains responsible for the animals.

## 9. Cancellation

- Cancel at least 24 hours before the start of the stay for a full refund.
- Later cancellations are billed in full, unless Porchlight or the sitter cancels, in which case the owner receives a full refund and Porchlight will try to find a replacement sitter.
- Holiday stays may carry the published holiday charge.

## 10. Price

The price is the published overnight price, plus any additional-animal charges and any published holiday charge, shown before you pay. A tip is optional and goes entirely to the sitter.
