Pet care in Newport

Newport is a census-designated place in York County, South Carolina. Most of it has been annexed into the city of Rock Hill, though parts remain in unincorporated York County. It covers about eight square miles and had 4,744 residents in 2020, up about 15 percent since 2010. The median age is about 42, and nearly two in three households are married couples, which describes a lot of households with dogs, cats and busy schedules.

Newport is near the southern end of our service area. That is by design: it keeps us west of the lake and out of the I-85 and Charlotte-bound traffic that eats a sitter’s day. We plan visits here in blocks, so we recommend booking a standing weekly plan if you want the same time every week.

Rock Hill itself is a good place to walk a dog. The Piedmont Medical Center Trail follows the Catawba River on a shaded, paved path that the York County visitors bureau recommends for high-energy dogs, and downtown Rock Hill has grassy spots, shops that welcome dogs and a brewery known for its dog-friendly ice cream.

About pet care in Newport

Is Newport inside your service area?

Yes, it is at the southern end of the area. We still check your exact address against our border and drive time when you book.

Can I book a standing weekly walk?

Yes. You can set a visit to repeat on the days you choose, which also gives us the best chance of keeping a steady time slot on your street.

Do you take pets to the vet from Newport?

Yes. The pet taxi picks up at your home and takes your pet to the vet, groomer or any place they need to go. It is priced by the mile, and you see the fare before you book.
